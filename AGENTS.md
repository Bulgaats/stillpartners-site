# Office Manager development

For Office Manager work, read docs/OFFICE_MANAGER_DIRECTION.md and docs/office-manager.md before implementation. The former records the owner-approved product direction and delivery priorities; its proposed features and check cadences are not claims of current capability or blanket execution permission. Preserve the distinction between proposals, approved decisions, attempts and verified outcomes. Follow existing access, financial-evidence, source-preservation and exact-message approval controls. Keep progress and stopping points explicit to the owner.

This is a public repository. Never commit credentials, company correspondence, invoice sources, private snapshots or real-person test fixtures.
